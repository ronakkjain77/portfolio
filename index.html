01 Making Google's logistics dashboards fast enough to act on Quilytics · client project for Google (xWF) · Logistics Analyst ~70% faster

The problem

Google's logistics teams were mid-migration. Dashboards were slow enough that people stopped opening them, and the KPIs on those dashboards had drifted into operational trivia — numbers that moved without telling anyone what to do.

What I did

  • Led query optimisation and dashboard engineering across the reporting layer.
  • Built new metrics aligned to actual business goals, replacing arbitrary operational KPIs.
  • Shipped critical feature requests and metric enhancements so the dashboards matched how teams really worked.
  • Turned around ad-hoc analysis and bug fixes fast enough to keep migration timelines intact.

Outcome

Dashboard performance improved by roughly 70%, putting data within near real-time reach. The migration landed smoothly and operations stayed stable afterwards — the part nobody notices when it goes right.

  • SQL
  • Query optimisation
  • Dashboard engineering
  • KPI design
  • Stakeholder alignment
02 Marketing mix modeling across multiple client accounts Quilytics · Data Analyst Spend strategy

The problem

Clients were allocating marketing budget on last-click attribution and habit. Channels that assisted but didn't close were invisible, so they were systematically underfunded.

What I did

  • Built marketing mix models for multiple clients to measure true channel effectiveness.
  • Separated genuine channel contribution from baseline and seasonal effects.
  • Translated model output into spend recommendations a marketing lead could act on without reading a regression table.

Outcome

Budget conversations moved from opinion to evidence. Clients could see which channels were carrying the result and reallocate accordingly.

  • Marketing Mix Modeling
  • Regression
  • Python
  • Time series
  • Client reporting
03 Budget pacing that warns you before the money's gone Quilytics · Data Analyst Automation

Pacing was checked manually and retrospectively. By the time anyone noticed a campaign was overspending or underspending, the window to correct it had closed.

What I did

  • Built the alerting so drift surfaced while there was still budget left to steer.

Outcome

Spend control became proactive. The team stopped writing post-mortems about budget and started adjusting mid-flight.

05 Turning open-text survey answers into something you can query Viral Fission · Business Intelligence Analyst NLP

The problem

Users answered surveys in free text. That's the richest data in the product and the least usable — thousands of responses nobody could aggregate.

What I did

  • Wrote a named entity recognition program in Jupyter to pull structured fields out of open-text responses.
  • Added sentiment analysis to classify whether a response was positive or negative about the question asked.
  • Built auto-tagging logic so new surveys classified themselves, and used OpenAI to drive campaign-assignment logic.
  • Designed the points system scoring Youth Ambassadors and Team Leaders across each IP in the app, and validated the automated allocation algorithm against the intended logic.
  • Moved reporting onto BigQuery and GA4, with Tableau on top.

Outcome

Free text became a queryable dimension. Performance scoring stopped being manual, and the dashboards finally reflected what people actually wrote.

  • NER
  • Sentiment analysis
  • Python
  • BigQuery
  • GA4
  • Tableau
  • OpenAI API
